Discover the story behind your seafood

  • Musholm is a Danish trout producer, farming across three sea farms and 18 freshwater farms. Our approach follows the trout’s natural life cycle, from spring water to the open sea.

  • We raise trout in stages that mirror the fish’s own life cycle: the eggs hatch in spring water, the fish grow in freshwater and they are then transferred to saltwater at sea.

Musholm is an ASC-certified aquaculture producer

The Aquaculture Stewardship Council (ASC) sets internationally recognised standards for responsible fish farming, covering environmental, animal welfare and social responsibilities.

Musholm has implemented the ASC standard at Musholm Vest, where the majority of production takes place. This certification reflects a commitment to responsible aquaculture and to producing fish with care for the environment, animal welfare and the people involved.

Making history as the first aquaculture company to create Science Based Targets for Nature

Through participation in the Science Based Targets Network (SBTN) Ocean Hub pilot project, Musholm is taking the next step in its nature environmental efforts.

Read the full press release

1. Reducing pressure on wild fish stocks

Together with BioMar, Musholm has set a target to cut its use of marine raw materials from blue whiting by 52.94% by 2029, against a 2025 baseline. By 2031 at the latest, reliance on wild-caught marine raw materials falls further through greater use of circular and alternative ingredients — with progress documented through BioMar's sustainability analyses and the Blue Food Performance FIFO tool.

2. Restoring marine habitats in the Danish Great Belt

Musholm aims to reduce the use of net impregnation at its three sea farms every year through 2030. Alongside this, the company is helping to finance and deliver the Great Belt Marine Nature Restoration Project, which aims to restore and protect 42 hectares of rock reef and 14 hectares of blue mussel reef by the end of 2029.

3. Protecting threatened and protected species

By 2029 at the latest, Musholm and BioMar will strengthen traceability, monitoring and mitigation in selected fisheries in the North Sea and South America that supply marine raw materials for feed. Participation in Fishery Improvement Projects supports responsible fisheries management and better interactions between endangered, threatened, and protected (ETP) species and fishing gear.

What is the Science Based Targets Network (SBTN)?

The Science Based Targets Network develops science-based methods and guidance that helps companies and cities set credible targets and take action to halt and reverse nature loss.

Trout fed with BioMar’s Blue Impact

Blue Impact is BioMar’s holistic feed concept for improving and documenting the feed-related sustainability of farmed seafood. Powered by BioSustain™, it measures progress across three key areas: carbon footprint, circular and restorative raw materials, and reliance on finite wild fish resources.

What is Blue Impact?

The Blue Impact feed program supports fish farmers who want greater transparency and continuous improvements in the environmental footprint of their feed.

Impact Parameters

BioMar uses three impact parameters to guide the development of tailored feed formulations for Musholm, helping turn its nature ambitions into measurable environmental progress.

Circular & Restorative

Circular & Restorative

The percentage of circular and restorative ingredients is a measure of the level of by-products (circularity) and vegetable ingredients from regenerative agriculture (restorative).

Carbon footprint

Carbon Footprint

The total amount of greenhouse gas emissions produced to farm 1 kg of feed, expressed as carbon dioxide equivalents.

Forage Fish dependency

Forage Fish dependency

The total amount of forage fish needed to produce one kilogram of farmed fish (i.e. FFDR). A ratio below 1:1 indicates net fish production.
