Raised on an ASC-certified farm

Musholm Vest, where most of our trout is raised, is certified to the Aquaculture Stewardship Council (ASC) standard.

ASC sets internationally recognised standards for fish farming. They cover the environment, the welfare of the fish, and the people who work on the farm.

The first fish farmer with Science Based Targets for Nature

Our fish depend on healthy seas, both where we farm and where their feed comes from. So we have set three targets to protect nature, as part of the Science Based Targets Network's Ocean Hub pilot.

Read the full press release

1. Less wild fish in our feed

We are cutting the blue whiting in our feed by more than half, and using more by-products and alternative ingredients in place of wild-caught fish.

By 2029

2. Restoring reefs in the Great Belt

We are helping to restore 42 hectares of rock reef and 14 hectares of blue mussel reef, around 80 football pitches. We are also cutting the anti-fouling treatment on our sea farm nets every year.

By the end of 2029

What is the Science Based Targets Network?

An organisation that helps companies and cities set credible targets to halt and reverse the loss of nature.

Learn more

3. Protecting sea life where feed is fished

Through fishery improvement projects in the North Sea and South America, we are working to protect threatened species from fishing gear.

By 2029

How to cook your trout

Trout is mild and quick to cook. Here are two easy ways to enjoy it.

Crispy pan-fried trout

10 minutes · Serves 2
  1. Pat 2 fillets dry and season with salt and pepper.
  2. Heat a little oil in a pan over medium-high heat. Fry skin side down for 4 minutes, until the skin is crisp.
  3. Turn, add a knob of butter, and cook for 1 more minute.
  4. Finish with lemon and fresh dill. Serve with new potatoes.

Oven-baked trout with vegetables

15 minutes · Serves 2
  1. Heat the oven to 200°C.
  2. Lay 2 fillets on a tray with sliced fennel, cherry tomatoes and a drizzle of olive oil.
  3. Bake for 10–12 minutes, until the fish flakes easily.

Storing: keep in the fridge and eat by the date on the pack.

Is it done? The flesh turns opaque and separates easily with a fork.
