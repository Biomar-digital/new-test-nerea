Discover the story behind your trout

We raise our trout in stages that follow the fish's own life cycle, from the spring water where the eggs hatch to the open sea.

1

Spring water

The eggs hatch in spring water.

2

Freshwater

The young trout grow up in our 18 freshwater farms in Denmark.

3

The sea

They then move to one of our three sea farms in the Great Belt.

Who we are

Musholm is a Danish trout producer based at Reersø harbour on west Zealand, on the Great Belt. We farm across 18 freshwater farms and three sea farms, and follow each trout from the egg all the way to the sea.

We care about the waters we farm in, and the places our feed comes from. That is why we were the first fish farmer to set Science Based Targets for Nature.

“We want to take responsibility locally, where we raise our fish, but also in the parts of the world where the ingredients for our feed come from, because our demand also affects nature there.”

— Thorbjørn Harkamp, CEO of Musholm
