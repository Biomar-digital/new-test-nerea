What our trout eats

Feed is one of the biggest parts of a farmed fish's environmental footprint. Our trout eat Blue Impact feed from BioMar, which measures the footprint of every feed so it can keep improving it.

Get full list of ingredients

Three numbers we track

BioMar measures three things in our feed, and uses them to improve it year-by-year. Hover over or tap each one below to see where it sits on the scale.

Carbon footprint

1.44 kg CO₂e of greenhouse gas emissions for every kg of feed produced.

Circular & restorative

About a third (32.6%) of the feed is made from by-products and from crops grown with regenerative farming.

Wild fish in the feed

About 1.1 kg of wild forage fish goes into the feed for each kg of trout we raise. We are working to bring this down.
