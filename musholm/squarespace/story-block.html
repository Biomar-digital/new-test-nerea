Who we are

Musholm is a Danish trout producer based at Reersø harbour on west Zealand, on the Great Belt. We farm across 18 freshwater farms and three sea farms, and follow each trout from the egg all the way to the sea.

We care about the waters we farm in, and the places our feed comes from. That is why we were the first fish farmer to set Science Based Targets for Nature.
